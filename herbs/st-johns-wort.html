St. John's Wort

Hypericum perforatum

Hypericaceae (St. John's Wort family)

Overview

St. John's Wort is a flowering plant with a long history of use in traditional medicine for mental health conditions.

Traditional Uses

Historically used for anxiety, depression, and wound healing since ancient Greek times.

Active Compounds

Hypericin and hyperforin are the primary active compounds responsible for its medicinal activity.

Potential Benefits — Unverified Summary

Extensively studied for mild-to-moderate depression; may also support menopausal symptoms and PMS.

Safety & Side Effects

High potential for drug interactions. Can reduce the effectiveness of birth control and blood thinners. Consult a doctor before use.

When to See a Doctor

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience:

  • Severe allergic reactions (difficulty breathing, swelling of face or throat, severe rash)
  • Chest pain or irregular heartbeat
  • Severe digestive issues (persistent vomiting, bloody stools)
  • Severe headache or dizziness
  • Any symptoms that worsen or do not improve

Always consult a healthcare professional before using herbal supplements if you:

  • Are pregnant, nursing, or planning to become pregnant
  • Are taking prescription medications
  • Have a chronic health condition
  • Are scheduled for surgery
  • Are giving herbs to children
